# Credits

We are the company about standing on other people's shoulders, so we name
ours. Every third-party asset or library this film ships with is listed here
and on the site's credits page, with its license checked per file before
release.

## In use

- three.js (MIT)
- Lenis by darkroom.engineering (MIT)
- Vite (MIT)

## Planned, not yet shipped

- 1903 Wright Flyer photogrammetry scan, Smithsonian Institution, public
  domain (CC0)
- Apollo-era boot, A7L suit, and flag assembly models, NASA 3D resources,
  stated policy free of copyright; verify per file before shipping
- EZ-Tree by Dan Greenheck (MIT), if the procedural tree is replaced

## In hand

Raw scans downloaded and staged under public/assets/raw, which is gitignored
and license checked per file. Full terms and sizes are in
public/assets/ASSETS.md.

- 1903 Wright Flyer, Smithsonian Institution, National Air and Space Museum, CC0
  public domain dedication, credit line the Estate of Orville Wright
- Astronaut figure, NASA 3D Resources, free of copyright per the repository
  readme and NASA media usage guidelines
- Extravehicular Mobility Unit pressure suit, NASA 3D Resources, free of
  copyright, staged as an alternative figure
- Apollo lunar module, NASA 3D Resources, free of copyright, staged as an
  optional Moon scene prop
- IM Fell English and Hanken Grotesk, self-hosted woff2, SIL Open Font
