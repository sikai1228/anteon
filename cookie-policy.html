Cookie policy

Effective July 15, 2026.

This site sets no cookies. This policy explains the two small values it keeps in your browser instead, both functional, neither sent anywhere.

No cookies, no trackers

The site’s own code sets no cookies, not for analytics, not for advertising, not for anyone else. It loads no analytics scripts and no tracking pixels, and every font and asset is served from the site itself, so no advertising network hears about your visit.

What the site stores instead

The site keeps two values in your browser’s storage. Both stay on your device, neither identifies you, and neither is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • anteon-lang, in local storage. Your language choice, English or Spanish, so the site opens in it next time. It stays until you clear your browser data or pick the other language.
  • anteon-scroll, in session storage. Your place in the film on the home page, so a reload in the same tab resumes where you left off. Your browser discards it when the tab closes.

How this storage differs from cookies

Local storage and session storage live only in your browser. Unlike a cookie, they are not attached to the requests your browser makes, so they never travel to a server, ours or anyone’s.

How to clear them

Clearing this site’s data in your browser settings removes both values. The site keeps working; it just forgets your language and your place in the film.

Changes to this policy

If the site ever needs a cookie or a new stored value, we will update this policy first and post a new effective date.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, reach us through the contact page. For what we collect when you talk to us, read the privacy policy.
