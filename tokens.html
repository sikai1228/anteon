Design tokens

One name for every value the shell uses, all served from tokens.css. The film keeps its chalk look in LOOK; everything outside the board reads from here.

Color

Two grounds, two inks, and the flag pair. The red is the film's single color moment and the shell's accent; accent is earned, so it colors links, focus, and small marks, never grounds.
